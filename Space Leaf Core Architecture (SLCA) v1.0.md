Space Leaf Core Architecture (SLCA) v1.0
Status
Draft Foundation Standard
Organization
Space-LEAF Corporation
Purpose
The Space Leaf Core Architecture (SLCA) defines the universal framework, standards, interfaces, governance principles, safety systems, and interoperability requirements that govern all Space-LEAF technologies.
SLCA serves as the root architecture for:
* Space Car
* Digital University
* Environment Package Specification (EPS)
* Agent Package Specification (APS)
* Artificial Intelligence Systems
* Simulation Platforms
* Education Platforms
* Future Hardware Systems
* Future Virtual Environments
No Space-LEAF product shall operate outside the SLCA framework.
 
⸻
 
1. Core Mission
Space-LEAF exists to create safe, educational, human-centered environments that expand learning, exploration, creativity, and collaboration.
Every system shall prioritize:
1. Human Safety
2. Child Safety
3. Transparency
4. Education
5. Accessibility
6. Privacy
7. User Agency
8. Interoperability
9. Long-Term Sustainability
 
⸻
 
2. Architectural Principles
Principle 1 — Safety First
Safety supersedes functionality.
If a conflict exists between a feature and user safety, safety prevails.
 
⸻
 
Principle 2 — Human Agency
Users retain decision authority.
AI systems assist.
AI systems do not govern.
 
⸻
 
Principle 3 — Modular Design
Every system shall be modular.
Components must be replaceable without requiring reconstruction of the entire ecosystem.
 
⸻
 
Principle 4 — Open Standards
Interfaces, schemas, and protocols shall be documented and versioned.
 
⸻
 
Principle 5 — Family Compatibility
All systems shall support family-safe operational modes.
 
⸻
 
Principle 6 — Educational Utility
Every environment should provide opportunities for learning and personal growth.
 
⸻

9. Child and Family Protection
Mandatory requirements:
* Guardian Controls
* Content Filtering
* Environment Certification
* AI Safety Constraints
* Privacy Preservation

* AI Companion Ecosystems

16. Foundational Rule
Every world, vehicle, agent, service, tool, simulation, and future platform created under Space-LEAF shall inherit its operational standards from SLCA.
SLCA is the root architecture from which all ecosystem specifications derive.
